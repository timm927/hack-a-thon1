📂 Projects Overview
1. World Bank – Debt & Bond Market Risk Analysis

Tools: Python, Excel, World Bank datasets, visualization libraries.

Key Highlights:

Built risk models to simulate repayment scenarios.

Created visualizations showing trade-offs between borrowing, interest, and economic growth.

Developed actionable insights for policy-level decision-making.


2. Loan Portfolio Risk Analysis

Objective: Estimate credit risk and present value of expected cash flows for a 10-year loan portfolio.

Tools: Python, Excel.

Key Highlights:

Converted annual PD to monthly probabilities and merged with loan cash flow schedules.

Calculated expected portfolio cash flows under different discount rate scenarios.

Conducted stress tests and sensitivity analyses to inform risk management decisions.
