# Quantium Retail Analytics Project – Chips Category

## 📌 Overview
This project is part of a retail analytics case study with **Quantium**. 

The goal was to analyze chip purchase behavior, test trial store layouts, and provide commercial recommendations to guide the client’s strategic plan for the next half year.

The work was divided into **three tasks**:

1. **Data Preparation & Customer Analytics**
    
2. **Experimentation & Uplift Testing**
   
3. **Analytics & Commercial Application**

---

## 🗂️ Task 1: Data Preparation & Customer Analytics

- Cleaned and validated **transaction** and **customer** datasets.
  
- Merged both datasets for analysis.
  
- Defined key metrics such as:
  
  - **Total sales**
    
  - **Number of customers**
    
  - **Average units per transaction**
    
- Created visualizations (e.g., *Total Sales by Segment*) to highlight customer trends.
    
- **Insights**:
  
  - Chips transactions spike significantly before **Christmas**, showing opportunity for promotions.
      
  - **Mainstream Young Singles & Couples** are the primary chip shoppers.
    
  - **Young and Older Families** (26% of shoppers) buy larger baskets, offering room for growth.  

---

## 🗂️ Task 2: Experimentation & Uplift Testing

- Evaluated trial layouts in **stores 77, 86, and 88**.
  
- Selected **control stores** for each trial using similarity measures (sales, customer counts, transactions).
  
- Compared trial vs control stores at a **monthly level**.
  
- Assessed whether differences were **statistically significant**.
  
- **Insights**:
  
  - Trial stores showed **strong uplift** compared to controls, especially Feb–May.
    
  - Improvement was driven by **increased customer numbers** and higher transactions per customer.  

---

## 🗂️ Task 3: Analytics & Commercial Application

- Structured client report using the **Pyramid Principle**:
  
  1. Start with recommendations.
       
  2. Support with key insights.
     
  3. Back up with detailed analysis.
     
      
- Prepared a professional presentation with:
  
  - Data visualizations
    
  - Key callouts
    
  - Actionable recommendations
      
- **Recommendations**:
  
  - Roll out the **new store layout** across more locations given proven success.
     
  - Use **seasonal promotions** (e.g., gondola ends before Christmas) to boost holiday sales.
      
  - Target **Young & Older Families** alongside **Mainstream Young Singles & Couples** to expand category growth.
