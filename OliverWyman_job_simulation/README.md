🌍# Oliver Wyman Climate Change Job Simulation  

This project is based on the **Oliver Wyman Financial Services: Climate Change Job Simulation** completed on the Forage platform in September 2025.  
It focuses on understanding **financed emissions**, exploring **target metrics**, analyzing **net-zero scenarios**, and providing **strategic recommendations** for a client bank (Bank ABC).  

---

## 📌 Project Overview  

Financial institutions play a big role in climate change because the companies they finance (through loans and investments) produce greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions.

In this simulation, I acted as a consultant on Oliver Wyman’s **Climate and Sustainability team** to help a client bank:  

1. Measure its **financed emissions**.
   
2. Explore possible **metrics** to set climate targets.
    
3. Recommend the best **net-zero pathway** for the bank.
    
4. Estimate **2030 emissions reduction targets** and identify levers (like renewables investment) to achieve them.  

---

## 🛠️ Key Tasks  

### 1. Financed Emissions  

- Learned about **Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions** under the Greenhouse Gas Protocol.
   
- Classified **financed emissions** as part of Scope 3 (the largest and hardest to track).
   
- Understood why measuring financed emissions is critical for banks to manage climate risks and opportunities.  

### 2. Target Metrics  

- Explored and compared different metrics:
  
  - **Absolute Financed Emissions (AFE):** total emissions linked to the bank’s lending/investments.
    
  - **Physical Emissions Intensity (PEI):** emissions per unit of physical output (e.g., per barrel of oil).
     
  - **Financed Emissions Lending Intensity (FELI):** emissions per unit of financial exposure.
      
- Recommended **PEI** as the most practical metric for this case.  

### 3. Net-Zero Scenario Recommendation  

- Researched three main scenarios:
  
  - **IEA Net Zero 2050 (NZE)**
    
  - **NGFS Net Zero 2050**
    
  - **NGFS Divergent Net Zero**
    
- Recommended **IEA Net Zero 2050** as the most realistic pathway for Bank ABC, balancing:
   
  - Transition costs
    
  - Policy differences across regions
    
  - Risks for high-emission clients  

### 4. 2030 Target Setting & Strategy  

- Calculated Bank ABC’s **baseline emissions footprint** using PEI.
   
- Estimated **2030 preliminary reduction targets** based on IEA NZE pathways.
   
- Suggested **strategic levers** for achieving targets, such as:
  
  - Investing in **renewable energy projects**.
    
  - Supporting clients with **low-carbon transition financing**.
    
  - Shifting portfolios gradually away from **high-emission sectors**.
